高频语法结构先理解关系，再记形式
01 / TENSE

现在完成时

过去发生、对现在仍有影响，或从过去持续到现在。

have / has + past participle

I have worked here for three years.

“for three years” 强调动作延续至今。

02 / CLAUSE

非限定性定语从句

补充说明前面的名词或整句话，去掉后主干仍完整。

noun, which / who …,

The proposal, which was revised twice, was approved.

逗号不能省，which 不可替换为 that。

03 / CONDITION

虚拟条件句

表达与现在事实相反的假设及其结果。

If + past, would + verb

If I had more time, I would learn another language.

be 动词在正式表达中常统一使用 were。

04 / INVERSION

否定词前置倒装

Never、Rarely、Not only 等位于句首时，助动词提前。

Negative + auxiliary + subject + verb

Never have I seen such a clear explanation.

倒装发生在主句，注意助动词与时态匹配。

写作与口语句式替换关键词即可使用
01

It is widely acknowledged that…

It is widely acknowledged that consistent practice matters more than intensity.

陈述共识
02

What matters most is…

What matters most is how effectively we use the available resources.

突出重点
03

Not only…, but it also…

Not only does the policy reduce costs, but it also improves efficiency.

递进
04

There is growing evidence that…

There is growing evidence that urban green spaces improve well-being.

引出论据
05

From a practical perspective,…

From a practical perspective, the plan is both affordable and scalable.

转换视角
06

Were it not for…, …

Were it not for digital tools, the research would take much longer.

正式虚拟
